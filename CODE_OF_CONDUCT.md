# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be useful to people with different levels of experience and from different backgrounds. Be respectful, technical, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good faith unless there is clear evidence otherwise.
- Keep bug reports and reviews focused on reproducible behavior.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's credentials, private repository contents, or personal information.
- Follow coordinated disclosure practices for security-sensitive reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets, spam, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users. Security reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
